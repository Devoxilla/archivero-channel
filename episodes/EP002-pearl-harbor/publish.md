## Description
Japan's attack on Pearl Harbor is remembered as a total surprise. It wasn't. American codebreakers were reading Japan's secret diplomatic cables, Washington sent Hawaii a formal war warning ten days before the attack, and on the morning itself a destroyer sank a Japanese submarine and a radar station saw the planes coming. Tonight on Brinkman: the oil embargo that put Japan on a clock, Yamamoto's gamble, the Magic intercepts, the bomb plot message, the war warning that named every target except Hawaii, the carriers that vanished, the codes that burned, Japan's 14-part message, the warning sent by ordinary telegram, the radar blip at 7:02, and who was really to blame.

If you had been Admiral Kimmel on November 27, what would you have done? Tell me in the comments.
