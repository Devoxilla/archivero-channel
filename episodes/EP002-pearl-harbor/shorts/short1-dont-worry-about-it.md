[S02] THE BLIP {art: S01.2, S19.1, S01.1, S19.2, S01.4}
Seven o'clock, the 7th of December 1941. Two Army privates on Oahu kept their new radar running past shutdown, for practice. Then it lit up. Something huge, a hundred and thirty-seven miles north, and coming closer.

[S03] THE CALL {art: S19.3, S01.3, S19.4, S19.5}
They phoned it in. The only officer on duty, on his second shift, knew American B-17s were due from California. He said: don't worry about it.
