[S01] HOOK {art: S16.2, S05.3, S16.1}
The night before Pearl Harbor, the President read Japan's secret message.

[S02] THE CODE {art: S05.1, S05.2, S05.3, S16.1}
America had cracked Japan's diplomatic cipher. On the 6th of December 1941, Tokyo sent its ambassador a reply in fourteen parts. Thirteen were decoded that night and carried to the White House.

[S03] THIS MEANS WAR {art: S16.2, S16.3, S16.4, S07.2}
Roosevelt read them and said: this means war. But they named no place and no time. The top admiral was at the theater. Nobody phoned Hawaii.
