[S01] HOOK {art: S01.5, S03.4, S01.1}
Two men. Thirty minutes. One pencil line. And millions would die because of it.

[S02] THE MAP {art: S03.2, S03.3, S10.1!, S01.5, S03.4, S03.5}
August 1945. Soviet troops were pouring into northern Korea. In Washington, two officers, Charles Bonesteel and Dean Rusk, were told to propose a dividing line, fast. With a National Geographic map, they picked the 38th parallel, because it kept Seoul on the American side.

[S03] THE SURPRISE {art: S05.1, S04.3, S04.1, S04.2, S06.1, S19.1}
Stalin accepted it. A line meant to be temporary split one country into two states, and five years later into a war that killed millions.
