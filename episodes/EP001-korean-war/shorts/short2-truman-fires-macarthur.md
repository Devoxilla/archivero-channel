# Short 2 — The president who fired his most famous general
Title: Why Truman fired General MacArthur in the middle of a war #shorts
Pinned comment: How the Korean War became a limited war: full file on the channel.

[S01] HOOK {art: S15.1}
In April 1951, in the middle of a war, the American president fired his most famous general.

[S02] THE GENERAL {art: S09.1}
Douglas MacArthur had just pulled off the landing at Inchon. Now, with China in the war, he wanted to bomb Chinese bases, blockade China's coast, and he talked publicly about atomic weapons.

[S03] THE PRESIDENT {art: S15.2}
Harry Truman feared a third world war with the Soviet Union. When MacArthur openly challenged his policy, Truman removed him, on the 11th of April 1951.
