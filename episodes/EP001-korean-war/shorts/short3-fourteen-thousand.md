Fourteen thousand people. One cargo ship. Built for twelve passengers.

[S02] THE TRAP {art: S12.3}
December 1950. Chinese armies had just entered the Korean War. American Marines fought their way out of the frozen mountains at the Chosin Reservoir, down to the port of Hungnam.

[S03] THE EVACUATION {art: S12.4}
Tens of thousands of Korean civilians crowded the docks, desperate not to be left behind. The freighter SS Meredith Victory dumped its cargo and took them on board, packed into every hold and deck.
