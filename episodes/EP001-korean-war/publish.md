## Description
In 1945 two American officers had about thirty minutes to split Korea in two. Five years later, that line started a war that pulled in the United States, China and the Soviet Union, killed around three million people, and never officially ended. Tonight the Archivero opens the file: the division, Stalin's green light, the invasion, the Pusan Perimeter, Inchon, China's entry, the frozen Chosin Reservoir, Truman firing MacArthur, two years of talks and trench warfare, the armistice, and how the war still shapes East Asia today.

Was crossing the 38th parallel the biggest mistake of the war? Tell me in the comments.
