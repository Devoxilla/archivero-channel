# Short 2 — The Alcatraz escape nobody solved
Title: 3 men left fake heads in their beds and vanished #shorts

[S01] HOOK {art: S10.4}
In June 1962, guards at Alcatraz found three men asleep in their cells. Except they weren't men. They were papier-mâché heads.

[S02] THE ESCAPE {art: S10.5}
For months, they had widened the air vents in their cell walls with sharpened spoons and a drill built from a vacuum cleaner motor. That night they climbed to the roof, slid down to the shore, and paddled into San Francisco Bay on a raft made of raincoats.

[S03] PAYOFF {art: S10.3}
Officially, nobody ever escaped Alcatraz. But those three were never found. Thirty-six men tried, in fourteen attempts. Only five are still missing. The full file on every prison level is on the channel.
