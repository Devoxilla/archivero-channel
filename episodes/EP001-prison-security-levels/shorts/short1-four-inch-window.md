[S01] HOOK {art: S01}
This prison window is about four inches wide. And it was designed that way on purpose.

[S02] THE CELL {art: S12}
ADX Florence, Colorado. The most secure prison in America. Cells of roughly seven by twelve feet. A concrete bed, a concrete desk, a concrete stool. Most prisoners spend about twenty-three hours a day alone in there.

[S03] THE WINDOW {art: S12}
The one window is narrow and angled, so you can see the sky, but not the landscape. You cannot tell where in the building you are, or which way to run.
