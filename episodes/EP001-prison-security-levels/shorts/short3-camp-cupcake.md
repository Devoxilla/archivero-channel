# Short 3 — The prison with no fence
Title: Some US prisons don't have a fence. Here's why nobody leaves #shorts

[S01] HOOK {art: S05.1}
Some American prisons have no fence at all. So why doesn't everyone just walk out?

[S02] THE CAMP {art: S05.2}
Federal prison camps are minimum security. Dorms, not cells. Jobs in the kitchen or on the grounds. Martha Stewart served her sentence at one, and the tabloids called it Camp Cupcake.

[S03] PAYOFF {art: S05.4}
But walking away is a new federal crime. It adds years, and it sends you several levels up the ladder, where the fences are very real. The walls at a camp are mostly in your head.
