[S01] HOOK {art: S21.1}
The emperor Augustus banged his head against a door and shouted: "Quinctilius Varus, give me back my legions!"

[S02] THE TRAP {art: S20.4}
In 9 AD, a Roman army of three legions marched into a German forest. Their trusted ally Arminius had secretly united the tribes against them. For days, spears came out of the trees, in mud and rain.

[S03] THE LOSS {art: S20.7}
Almost the whole force was destroyed. The eagles were taken. The numbers Seventeen, Eighteen and Nineteen were never used for a Roman legion again.
