# Format: The Entire History of [X] Summarized (30–45 min, ~5,000–7,000 words at Kokoro pace)

[S01] HOOK — one shocking moment from the middle of the story, told in 3–4 sentences, then "this is how it got there."
[S02] INTRO — Brinkman: what X is, the date range, why it still matters today.
[S03] ORIGINS — geography, the people, the first ruler or founding myth (map shot).
[S04]–[S10] ERAS — one scene per era: what changed, the key ruler, the key war, one weird fact, one dark-funny line.
[S06] MID QUESTION — a question for the comments (around 40%).
[S11] PEAK — the height of power: size on the map, wealth, army.
[S12] FALL / TURN — what broke it.
[S13] TODAY — what is left, what it explains about the present.
[S14] OUTRO — one-line verdict, tease the next episode, "Next time on Brinkman..."
