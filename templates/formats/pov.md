# Format: POV: You're a [soldier] in [war] (25–40 min, second person "you")

[S01] HOOK — "It's [date]. You are [age], [place], and in [N] hours [terrible thing]."
[S02] INTRO — Brinkman: what this war was, how many served, how many came back.
[S03] RECRUITMENT — how you got here: volunteer, draft, poverty, propaganda.
[S04] TRAINING — what they teach you, what they don't.
[S05] KIT — uniform, weapon, food, pay (object shots).
[S06] DAILY LIFE — the boring 90%: marching, digging, waiting, disease.
[S07] MID QUESTION — for the comments.
[S08] FIRST BATTLE — the sensory version: noise, smell, confusion.
[S09] DISCIPLINE — punishments, officers, desertion.
[S10] THE WORST DAY — the famous disaster of this war.
[S11] ENDINGS — the ways it ends for you: killed, wounded, captured, discharged, statistics.
[S12] AFTER — veterans, land, pensions, trauma.
[S13] OUTRO — verdict + next episode.
