# Format: Every [X] Explained (20–35 min, a ranked ladder: least to most extreme)

[S01] HOOK — the most extreme item in 3 sentences.
[S02] INTRO — the Archivero: what the list is, how it's ordered.
[S03]–[S12] ONE ITEM PER SCENE — what it is, who gets it, how you earn it, one story, one weird fact (label shot + character shot).
[S07] MID QUESTION — "which one would you pick?"
[S13] OUTRO — the pattern behind the whole list + next file.
