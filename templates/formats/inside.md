# Format: How [Organisation] Worked From the Inside (25–35 min)

[S01] HOOK — the organisation's most infamous operation in 4 sentences.
[S02] INTRO — Brinkman: founded when, by whom, why.
[S03] RECRUITMENT — who they wanted and how they found them.
[S04] TRAINING — the tests, the schools, the dropout rate.
[S05] STRUCTURE — ranks, departments, the chain of command (diagram shot).
[S06] TOOLS — weapons, gadgets, methods.
[S07] MID QUESTION
[S08]–[S10] THREE OPERATIONS — one success, one disaster, one bizarre.
[S11] DAILY LIFE — pay, families, paranoia.
[S12] END OR TODAY — dissolution or the modern successor.
[S13] OUTRO
